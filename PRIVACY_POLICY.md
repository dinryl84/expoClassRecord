# Privacy Policy — SHS Class Record

**Last updated:** [fill in the date you publish this]

This policy describes how the SHS Class Record app ("the app") handles
information.

## Summary

The app does not collect, transmit, or share any data. Everything you
enter — learner names, scores, attendance, notes, and school information —
stays on your device, in a private local database. There are no analytics,
no advertising, no third-party trackers, and no server the app talks to.

## What the app stores, and where

The app stores the following, entirely on your device:

- Learner names, gender, and section/subject assignments
- Scores (Written Works, Performance Tasks, Summative Assessments)
- Attendance records
- Notes and observations you add about learners
- School information (region, division, school ID/name, teacher name,
  track, school year, term dates)
- A local username and password you set, used only to lock the app on
  this device (this is not an online account — it is never sent
  anywhere)

None of this is transmitted to the developer, to Anthropic, to Google, or
to any other party. The app has no backend server.

## Backups and exports

The app lets you:

- **Create a backup file** (JSON) of everything in the app, so you can
  restore it later or move it to another device
- **Import or export an Excel (.xlsx) class record** in the official DepEd
  ECR format
- **Print or save a PDF** of a class record

These files are created only when you choose to create them, and are
saved or shared only through the destination you pick (e.g. your device's
Files app, Google Drive, email, or another app you select from Android's
share sheet). The app itself does not upload these files anywhere.

## Permissions

The app does not request camera, location, contacts, microphone, or any
other sensitive device permission. It may ask to access the file system
only when you explicitly choose to import a file or save an export —
standard Android file-picker behavior, not background access.

## Data deletion

Because all data lives only on your device, you can delete it at any time
by:

- Deleting individual sections, learners, or records inside the app, or
- Uninstalling the app, which removes its local database entirely

There is no account to delete elsewhere, because no account or data ever
leaves your device.

## Children's data

This app is a tool for teachers to manage their own class records, which
may include the names of minor students. That data is entered by the
teacher, stored only on the teacher's own device, and is never transmitted
anywhere — the same privacy posture as a paper class record or a private
spreadsheet the teacher keeps. The app is not directed at children and is
not used by students.
